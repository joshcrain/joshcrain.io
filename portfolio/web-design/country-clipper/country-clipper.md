**Role:** Designer & Front-End Developer
**Tools:** HTML, CSS, JS/jQuery, Common Patterns Design System
**Scope:** Full project lifecycle — design through front-end development

Country Clipper makes zero-turn mowers. Their site needed to support lead generation and give their internal team the ability to update content without relying on outside help. Those two goals shaped every decision.

I was in the room from the first client meeting — understanding their brand, their internal capabilities, and what success looked like before any design work started. That early involvement mattered. A site their team couldn’t maintain confidently wasn’t a success, no matter how it looked.

I reviewed competitors and adjacent manufacturers to understand industry conventions and where Country Clipper could differentiate. Design was done in high-fidelity mockups, stitched into clickable prototypes for stakeholder feedback, then transitioned directly into responsive HTML and CSS. Front-end development was built on **Common Patterns**, my internal design system, with a customized layer for brand fit and styling.

The client maintained the site with pride for years after launch — which is the outcome that mattered most. Not the launch itself, but the sustained ownership.
